# Prompts para gerar cortes de cabelo (3D)

## Base (cole antes de cada estilo)

Low poly stylized anime hair mesh only, no head, no face, no body, no accessories. Single solid mesh with chunky thick strands (not thin ribbons), clean closed volumes, smooth rounded silhouette, flat shading friendly, no textures, neutral gray material, 2000 to 4000 triangles, T-pose neutral, front view, symmetrical, designed to fit a small chibi-proportioned head about 0.2 units wide, inspired by Ragnarok Online hairstyles.

## Estilos

1. Long straight: waist-length straight hair, flat blunt bangs (hime cut), two side locks framing the face, even tips.
2. Long wavy: shoulder-to-chest length soft waves, side-swept bangs, big rounded S-shaped locks.
3. Short bob: chin-length rounded bob, thick fringe over the forehead, slightly curved inward tips.
4. Pixie: very short cropped hair, short side-swept fringe, tiny nape tufts.
5. High ponytail: cap hair with one thick high ponytail tied at the crown, ponytail falling to mid-back, short bangs.
6. Side ponytail: one thick ponytail tied on the left side of the head, falling to the shoulder, curved tip, long side bang on the other side.
7. Braid: single thick braid down the back built as chunky segments, short bangs, small tie at the end.
8. Twin braids: two thick chunky braids falling in front of the shoulders, center-parted bangs.
9. Bun: single round top bun (sphere-like) at the crown, short bangs, two small side locks.
10. Messy short: short spiky messy hair, layered chunky tufts in different directions, asymmetric fringe.
11. Long spiky: shoulder-length hair with sharp chunky spikes, heroic wind-swept shape, big spiky bangs.
12. Curly afro-style: large rounded volume of chunky curls around the head, no strands longer than the volume, rounded silhouette.
13. Mohawk: shaved sides, a thick tall central crest from forehead to nape, chunky spikes.
14. Half-up: long straight hair with the top section tied in a small knot at the back of the crown, side locks free.
15. Dreadlocks: shoulder-length thick rope-like locks, 8 to 12 chunky cylinders hanging from a cap.
16. Long drill curls: two large spiral drill curls hanging on both sides (ojou style), straight bangs, cap on top.
17. Swept side: medium hair parted to one side, thick fringe covering one eye, tips flipped outward.
18. Cat ears hair: short bob with two pointed hair tufts on top shaped like cat ears, thick fringe.

## Dicas

- Se a ferramenta gerar fios finos, peca "thick volumes, solid blocks of hair, no hair cards".
- Gere sem cabeca: o mesh de cabelo deve ser uma casca que cobre a cabeca (aberta embaixo).
- Depois e so me enviar os FBX: eu ajusto escala, origem, UV, material e metas no padrao.
