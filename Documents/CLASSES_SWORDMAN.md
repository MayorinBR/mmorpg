# Swordman — Class Reference

Source: [iRO Wiki Classic — Swordman](https://irowiki.org/wiki/Swordman), consulted September 2026. This document is the first of a planned per-class series (see `MMO_Project_GDD.docx`, Section 10.1) — it exists to keep the real Ragnarok Online reference and this project's own implementation status next to each other, so future class work doesn't need to re-derive it. **Wiki cross-check, 2026-10-08:** every value and formula below was re-checked against iRO Wiki (Renewal and Classic); the sourced tables, the corrected assumptions and the code-vs-wiki divergences live in `SWORDMAN_WIKI_REFERENCE_2026-10-08.md`, summarized in the "Wiki cross-check" section at the end of this document.

## Overview

Base class, no promotion yet available in this project (class evolution — e.g. Swordman to Knight/Crusader — is tracked as planned but undesigned; see `PlayerClassController.ChangeClass`). In classic Ragnarok Online, Swordman is a Human-only class that job-changes from Novice at the Izlude guild, with a Job Level cap of 50. Design identity: a durable melee fighter built around high ATK and HP rather than evasion — "offense as defense."

Currently implemented in this project: the basic-attack block chance (`PlayerBlockController`, an `IDamageModifier` hook — 25% chance, full negation by default, tunable per prefab), every skill below except Endure's flinch-resistance half (see its row), the Job Level 50 stat bonus (see below), and the weapon-vs-monster-size damage table (see below).

**Implemented this pass.** `ClassJobLevelBonusLookup.asset` grants this flat bonus automatically once Swordman reaches Job Level 50, folded into every derived stat by `JobBonusStatsView` (mirrors how `EquippedStatsView` folds in equipment bonuses) — separate from normal spendable stat points, matching real Ragnarok Online. Real RO grants this incrementally across multiple Job Level milestones; this project only has the final Job 50 total documented, so the bonus is a single cliff-edge grant at Job 50 rather than a staged curve — add intermediate breakpoints if the real per-level schedule gets sourced later. **Update, 2026-10-08: the real schedule is now sourced** (iRO Wiki, Swordman, "Job Bonuses"): STR +1 at Job Level 2, 14, 33, 40, 47, 49 and 50; AGI +1 at 30 and 46; VIT +1 at 6, 18, 38 and 42; DEX +1 at 10, 22 and 36; LUK +1 at 26 and 44; INT none (totals unchanged). Not yet implemented: `ClassJobLevelBonusLookup` still holds one breakpoint per class; the cumulative per-range table is in the reference document.

|---|---|---|---|
| **Bash** | Offensive, Lv 1–10 | ATK ×130–400%, +5–50% accuracy | **Implemented.** Single-target physical damage (`Bash.asset`), max level 5 (project-wide skill level cap, not RO's 10). Now also grants a level-scaling Hit-rating bonus on its own damage roll (`SkillDefinition.GetAccuracyBonus`), matching the real accuracy bonus — tuned to reach the wiki's +50 upper value at this project's max level. |
| **Magnum Break** | Offensive, Lv 1–10 | Fire AoE, ATK ×120–300% to a 5×5 area | **Implemented this pass.** New `MagnumBreak.asset`: Fire-element physical damage to every living target within a radius of the caster, needing no pre-selected target at all (`SkillDefinition.IsAreaOfEffect` / `AreaRadius`, `PlayerSkillCaster.TryCastAreaDamage`) — this project's first area-of-effect skill. Damage curve tuned to reach the wiki's 300% upper value at this project's max level (5). No icon yet (see the shared skill-icon sprite sheet gap already tracked in the GDD). |
| **Provoke** | Active, Lv 1–10 | +5–32% target ATK, −10–55% target DEF (a taunt/debuff) | **Implemented this pass.** New `Provoke.asset`: an Enemy-targeted `SkillEffectType.Buff` skill applying a timed ATK-up/DEF-down modifier to the current target via `BuffController` (`PlayerSkillCaster.TryCastBuff`). `HealthComponent` reads the DEF debuff through the same optional-hook slot as its other defensive-stat sources; `EnemyAttackState` reads the ATK buff directly off `EnemyController.Buffs`. Real RO's 30s duration doesn't scale with level (sourced from iRO Wiki); the ATK/DEF percentages are linearly compressed to reach the wiki's level-10 values (+32%/−55%) at this project's level 5 cap, the same convention used for Bash's accuracy bonus. |
| **Endure** | Active, Lv 1–10 | Resists flinching for up to 7 hits / 10–37s, +1–10 MDEF | **Partially implemented.** New `Endure.asset`: a Self-targeted `SkillEffectType.Buff` skill granting a timed flat MDEF bonus through the same `BuffController` used by Provoke — `HealthComponent.MagicalDefense` adds it directly. Duration is linearly compressed to reach the wiki's 37s at this project's level 5 cap, following the same convention as the ATK/DEF percentages above. The flinch-resistance half is **still not implemented**, deliberately: real RO's flinch is an interruption of an in-progress cast, and this project's skills have no cast time at all (`PlayerSkillCaster.TryCastSkill` resolves instantly) — there is nothing for a hit to interrupt yet, so building a flinch/stagger system now would have nothing real to suppress. Blocked on a cast-time/windup system that doesn't exist, a deeper prerequisite than "status effects" (item 4) alone covers. **Correction, 2026-10-08:** the wiki does not describe Endure's flinch as cast interruption. iRO Wiki Classic says it "removes the attack and movement delay that usually occurs when a character receives damage" (hit delay / damage motion), and neither wiki page mentions a 7-hit count, so that figure above is unsourced. The real prerequisite is a hit-reaction system (a short attack/movement lock when damaged), not cast time. Wiki values: duration `7 + 3 x level` s, MDEF `+level`, SP 10, cooldown 10s; all already matched by `Endure.asset`. |
| **Increase HP Recovery** | Passive, Lv 1–10 | Boosts natural HP regen and healing-item effectiveness by 10–100% | **Partially implemented this pass.** New `IncreaseHPRecovery.asset`: a `SkillEffectType.Passive` skill boosting the new natural-regen tick (see item 3 below) — `SkillDefinition.GetPassiveRegenBonus` / `PlayerPassiveSkillController.GetRegenMultiplier`, summed the same on-demand way as Sword Mastery's ATK bonus. Linearly compressed to reach the wiki's +100% at this project's level 5 cap (+20%/level). The healing-item-effectiveness half is **not implemented**: this project has no "use a consumable item" code path at all yet — `ItemDefinition.HealthRestore`/`ManaRestore` are data-only, read only by tooltip text — so there is nothing yet for this skill to boost on that side. **Update, 2026-10-08: no longer true.** `PlayerConsumableUser.TryConsume` now exists (instant or over-time HP/MP restore, removes one unit, clears debuffs for `CuresPoison` items), so the item half is unblocked: wiki value is +10% x level to HP-healing items (cumulative with the VIT bonus), i.e. multiply `HealthRestore` inside `RestoreOnce`. Also note the wiki models the skill as a flat tick, not a multiplier: `5 x level` HP plus `0.2% x level` of max HP every 10s while standing still, and it does not apply while moving. |
| **Sword Mastery** | Passive, Lv 1–10 | +4–40 ATK with swords and daggers | **Implemented this pass.** New `SwordMastery.asset`: a `SkillEffectType.Passive` skill (`PlayerPassiveSkillController` sums it into `PlayerStatsController.CurrentSubStats.StatusAtk` whenever learned) that only applies while the equipped main-hand weapon's new `ItemDefinition.WeaponSubtype` is Dagger or One-Hand Sword (`SkillDefinition.PassiveRequiredWeaponSubtypes`). | **Berserk** (quest skill, Job Lv 30) | Passive | +32% ATK, −55% DEF while under 25% HP | **Implemented this pass.** New `Berserk.asset`: a `SkillEffectType.Passive` skill whose ATK/DEF values reuse the Buff system's fields (`buffAtkPercentPerLevel`/`buffDefPercentPerLevel`) rather than the flat passive-ATK field, since its effect is a percentage modifier, not a flat Status ATK bonus. A new `PlayerBerserkController` watches `HealthComponent.HealthChanged` and applies/clears the modifier via `BuffController.SetPersistentModifier` — a persistent (not timed) entry, so repeated HP changes replace rather than stack it. `PlayerStatsController.CurrentSubStats` now also multiplies Status ATK by any attached `BuffController.AttackMultiplier`, a gap this uncovered: player ATK previously never read any buff (enemies already did, via `EnemyAttackState`). Real RO's Job Level 30 unlock requirement isn't modeled — this project has no Job Level unlock gate anywhere yet — so learning the skill is the only requirement (quest items per the wiki: 10 Decayed Nail, 10 Honey, 10 Horrendous Mouth, 35 Butterfly Powder, NPC Juan at the Prontera Upgrade Shop; the Renewal page lists it as an active toggle, the Swordman page and Classic as passive, and the trigger is HP below 25%). | **Fatal Blow** (quest skill, Job Lv 30) | Passive | Bash (Lv 6–10) gains a chance to stun | **Implemented this pass.** New `FatalBlow.asset`: a `SkillEffectType.Passive` skill referencing Bash through a new `SkillDefinition.AugmentsSkill` field. `PlayerSkillCaster.TryProcStunAugment` rolls this chance whenever a landed Bash hit finds a learned passive augmenting it, scaling by **Bash's** current level (not Fatal Blow's own — Fatal Blow is single-rank, `maxLevel` 1), and applies the stun via the target's new `StatusEffectController`. Real RO scales 5–25% across Bash levels 6–10 and requires Bash Lv 6 plus a Job 30 quest; this project's Bash only reaches level 5, so the same linear-compression convention used elsewhere (Bash's own accuracy bonus, Provoke, Endure) applies 5% per Bash level starting at level 1, reaching 25% at this project's cap, and the quest/Job-Level gate isn't modeled, matching Berserk. Real RO's Job Level 35 unlock requirement isn't modeled, matching Berserk and Fatal Blow. **Wiki, 2026-10-08:** without this skill there is no natural regen while moving; with it, regen while moving runs at half the standing rate (12s tick instead of 6s) and the Increase HP Recovery extra only applies once standing still. Weapon-vs-monster-size damage scaling is also real-RO Swordman behavior (relevant to its dagger/one-hand-sword/two-hand-sword kit specifically) and is **implemented this pass** — see item 5 below; it's a general combat mechanic, not Swordman-specific code, so it applies to every class's weapon the moment any of them get equipped.

## Missing Systems (grouped — one build unblocks several skills at once)

1. ~~Passive skill effects~~ — **implemented this pass.** `SkillEffectType` gained a `Passive` value (never cast — `PlayerSkillCaster.TryCastSkill` and the Skill Book's hotbar drag both refuse it); `PlayerPassiveSkillController` sums every learned passive's bonus on demand. Sword Mastery and Two-Handed Sword Mastery are the first skills to use it. Still doesn't unblock Increase HP Recovery or HP Recovery While Moving on its own — see item 3 below, still needed for those. Berserk (see the Skill Roster above) combines this with item 2's buff system instead, and is now implemented.
2. ~~Buffs/debuffs~~ — **implemented this pass.** `SkillEffectType` gained a `Buff` value; new `BuffController` holds temporary, timed ATK%/DEF%/MDEF modifiers on whichever character it's attached to, read by `HealthComponent` (defense) and `EnemyController`/`EnemyAttackState` (attack) the same optional-hook way equipment and Job Level bonuses already are. Provoke (Enemy-targeted) and Endure (Self-targeted, MDEF half only) are the first skills to use it — see the Skill Roster above. `BuffController` also gained `SetPersistentModifier`, a non-timed entry keyed by source that replaces rather than stacks — needed for Berserk, a condition-triggered (not cast) buff that would otherwise add a new timed entry every time its HP-threshold trigger re-evaluates. Reusable as-is for the other five classes' own buffs/debuffs once designed.
3. ~~HP regen-over-time~~ — **implemented this pass.** New `PlayerHealthRegenController` (Project.Character.Combat, player-only — enemies don't learn skills, so they were left out rather than generalizing this onto `HealthComponent` for every character) heals a percentage of max health every fixed interval, boosted by `PlayerPassiveSkillController.GetRegenMultiplier()` (Increase HP Recovery) and halved while `CharacterMovementController.IsMoving` unless `IgnoresMovementRegenPenalty()` (HP Recovery While Moving) says otherwise. Taking a hit resets the tick timer instead of tracking a separate grace period, approximating Ragnarok Online's own "no regen right after being hit" behavior with one field instead of two. The interval (10s) and per-tick percentage (1% of max HP) are invented approximations. **Wiki, 2026-10-08:** natural HP regen is every 6s standing and 3s sitting, restoring `max(1, floor(MaxHP/200)) + floor(VIT/5)` (not AGI-dependent, which corrects this sentence's earlier claim); natural SP regen is every 8s standing and 4s sitting, restoring `1 + floor(MaxSP/100) + floor(INT/6)`. Overweight (70%+) cancels both, already implemented. Unblocks both Increase HP Recovery (regen half only, see the Skill Roster above) and HP Recovery While Moving.
4. ~~Status effects~~ — **implemented this pass, stun only.** New `StatusEffectController` holds a single timed stun (`IsStunned` / `ApplyStun`, extending rather than shortening an existing one), wired as the same kind of optional hook `BuffController` already is on both the player and `EnemyController`. `EnemyController.Update()` is the single choke point gating all enemy behavior during a stun — `NavMeshAgent.isStopped` forced true and the state machine's `Tick` skipped entirely — rather than patching each `IEnemyState` individually. Unblocks Fatal Blow (see the Skill Roster above) via a new `SkillDefinition.AugmentsSkill` / `GetStunChance` mechanism: a passive that adds a status-chance to a specific active skill, general enough to reuse for a similar quest-skill passive on another class later without being a heavyweight framework. A related but distinct gap remains: a hit-reaction/stagger (flinch) mechanic still doesn't exist, so Endure's "resists flinching" half stays unimplemented — a landed hit never flinches the player today regardless, so there's nothing yet for Endure to suppress. **Status Effect Resistance implemented, 2026-09-23** (full writeup in `CLASSES_SHARED_SYSTEMS_PLAN.md` item 1): a target's chance to fully resist an incoming status now lives inside `StatusEffectController.ApplyStun` itself, so Fatal Blow's stun-augment proc (`TryProcStunAugment`, which calls `ApplyStun` directly rather than through the shared `Apply()` dispatcher) is covered by the same VIT-derived roll as every other status source, not left as a separate unprotected path.
5. ~~Weapon subtypes + monster Size~~ — **implemented this pass, both halves.** Weapon subtypes cover every type on the [iRO Wiki weapon list](https://irowiki.org/wiki/Weapons): `ItemDefinition.WeaponSubtype` has Dagger, One/Two-Hand Sword, One/Two-Hand Axe, Mace, Spear, Bow, Book, Instrument, Whip, Rod, Knuckle, Katar, Gun and Huuma Shuriken, plus a project-specific `Shield` value for off-hand equipment with no attack of its own (this unblocked Sword Mastery vs. Two-Handed Sword Mastery). Monster Size is a new `CharacterStatsDefinition.Size` field (`MonsterSize`: Medium/Small/Large, Medium first so pre-existing assets default to it), and the weapon-vs-size damage table itself is a new `WeaponSizeModifiers.Apply(damage, category, weaponSubtype, targetSize)` — the single choke point both `PlayerCombatController.DealHit` (basic attacks) and `PlayerSkillCaster` (Physical skills, single-target and area) now call before `IDamageable.TakeDamage`, scaling only physical damage, leaving magical untouched, matching real Ragnarok Online. `IDamageable` gained a `Size` property so any attacker can read a target's size generically. Poring/Poporing (`PorigonStats.asset`/`PoporigonStats.asset`) are now explicitly tagged Small, matching real RO; every other existing enemy asset defaults to Medium (no penalty against a 1H sword, Swordman's most common weapon) until tagged otherwise.
6. ~~Class-specific Job Level stat bonuses~~ — **implemented this pass.** `ClassJobLevelBonusLookup` + `JobBonusStatsView` (see "Stat Bonuses at Job Level 50" above). Only Swordman's Job 50 entry is populated; add one per class as their real stat tables get researched.

Two gaps remain: Increase HP Recovery's healing-item-effectiveness half (**unblocked 2026-10-08**, since `PlayerConsumableUser` now exists; only the +10% x level hook in `RestoreOnce` is missing) and Endure's flinch-resistance half (needs a hit-reaction/damage-motion system, not cast time — see the correction in its row above). Both are blocked on a system this project doesn't have any other use for yet, unlike items 3 and 4, which had other skills (HP Recovery While Moving; Fatal Blow) to justify building the underlying system now.

## Wiki cross-check, 2026-10-08

Everything here is sourced from iRO Wiki (Renewal and Classic); full tables, formulas and proposed project values are in `SWORDMAN_WIKI_REFERENCE_2026-10-08.md`. No code or asset was changed in this pass.

Divergences between the current implementation and the wiki, in order of impact:

1. **Bash damage overshoots.** `Bash.asset` uses `damageMultiplierPerLevel: 1`, which gives 100% x level (500% at level 5). The wiki reaches 400% at level 10 (`100% + 30% x level`). Proposed 0.8, giving 400% at level 5. Accuracy bonus is right in magnitude (50 at level 5), but the wiki adds it as a percentage of the attacker's Hit rather than a flat value. SP is 8 for levels 1-5 and 15 for levels 6-10; the project uses 10 flat.
2. **Magnum Break is missing four wiki effects:** a +10% x level Hit bonus (100% at level 10), a 2-cell knockback, an HP cost (20 at level 1 down to 16 at level 9), and a 10-second Fire property buff (+20% Fire damage). SP is 30 in the wiki, 15 in the project. Damage curve (300% at the cap) is right.
3. **Provoke has no success roll and no immunities.** Wiki success rate is `50% + 3% x level` (53%-80%); Undead-property monsters and Boss monsters are immune. The project always succeeds. ATK/DEF values and the 30s duration are right; SP is `level + 3` in the wiki, 10 flat in the project.
4. **Enlarge Weight Limit is not scaled.** The skill adds 1000 per level raw, while base capacity is multiplied by `carryWeightScale` (0.05). Wiki: +200 per level.
5. **Natural regen formulas and the movement penalty** (see the Stats rows above).
6. **Size table:** Knuckle row is wrong (project 100/75/50, wiki 100/100/75); Huuma Shuriken is missing (wiki 75/75/100). All other weapons match, including every Swordman weapon.
7. **Job Level bonus curve** is now sourced but still delivered as one cliff-edge grant at Job 50.
8. **Quest skills** now have sourced requirements (Job Level 30 / 30 / 35, item lists, NPCs). Fatal Blow also requires Bash level 6 on the wiki page. The stun duration is not given by any wiki page, so the 3s value stays a placeholder, and Base Level scaling of the stun chance exists on the wiki without numbers.

Verified as matching the wiki: Sword Mastery and Two-Handed Sword Mastery (`4 x level`, weapon gating), Endure (duration, MDEF, SP, cooldown), Berserk (+32% ATK, -55% DEF, 25% threshold), Provoke ATK/DEF scaling, Magnum Break damage, weight job modifiers (Swordman 800) and the 70%/90% overweight thresholds.
